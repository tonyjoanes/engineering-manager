## Team Health Assessment

**When to Use:** You want to assess your team's overall health and identify areas for improvement.

**Related Resources:**
- [Team Health Metrics](../practices/team-health-metrics.md)
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
I want to assess the health of [team name].

Observable signals:
- DORA metrics: [deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate]
- Team dynamics: [collaboration quality, conflicts, morale]
- Recent retrospective themes: [common topics or complaints]
- Attrition: [recent departures or risk]
- Incident patterns: [frequency, types, post-mortems quality]
- Innovation: [when was the last improvement initiative?]

Help me:
1. Assess team health across key dimensions (delivery, collaboration, morale, quality)
2. Identify red flags or concerning patterns
3. Compare against healthy team benchmarks
4. Prioritize improvement areas
5. Create action plan for top 3 issues

Reference the team health metrics guide for frameworks (DORA, SPACE) and psychological safety guide.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Health assessment across multiple dimensions
- Red flags and areas of concern
- Prioritized improvement areas
- Action plan with specific interventions
- Metrics to track improvement

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Share assessment with team (where appropriate)
- Implement top 3 improvements
- Track metrics over time
- Regular health check-ins

## Psychological Safety Improvement

**When to Use:** Team members aren't speaking up, taking risks, or sharing ideas.

**Related Resources:**
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)
- [Retrospectives](../practices/retrospectives.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
I'm concerned about psychological safety on [team name].

Observable signs:
- [e.g., "people don't disagree in meetings" or "fear of asking questions" or "blame culture after incidents"]
- Recent examples: [specific incidents]
- Team size: [number]
- Team composition: [junior-heavy / mixed / senior-heavy]
- Recent changes: [new members, incidents, org changes]

Help me:
1. Assess current psychological safety level (Stage 1-4)
2. Identify root causes of low safety
3. Create plan to build psychological safety
4. Design team activities or changes
5. Measure improvement over time

Reference the psychological safety guide for the 4 stages and building strategies.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Current psychological safety assessment
- Root cause analysis
- Specific actions to build safety (team norms, leader behaviors, etc.)
- Timeline and milestones
- Measurement approach

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Model vulnerability as leader
- Implement team norms
- Practice inclusive behaviors
- Create safe spaces for risk-taking
- Measure with surveys

## Effective Retrospective Design

**When to Use:** You want to run a retrospective that's actually productive and leads to change.

**Related Resources:**
- [Retrospectives](../practices/retrospectives.md)
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
I need to facilitate a retrospective for [team name] covering [sprint / incident / quarter / etc.].

Context:
- What we're retrospecting on: [sprint, major project, incident, etc.]
- Team mood: [energized / frustrated / tired / mixed]
- Recent patterns: [same issues keep coming up / lots of change / etc.]
- Last retro: [what format we used, how it went]
- Specific goals: [improve process / address conflict / celebrate wins / etc.]

Help me:
1. Choose the right retrospective format for this context
2. Design the session structure and timing
3. Prepare questions to drive meaningful discussion
4. Plan how to ensure psychological safety
5. Create action item accountability system

Reference the retrospectives guide for different formats and best practices.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Recommended retro format with rationale
- Session agenda with timing
- Discussion questions
- Action item tracking approach
- Follow-up plan

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Send pre-work if needed
- Facilitate retrospective
- Document action items with owners
- Track completion
- Follow up in next retro

## Team Conflict Pattern

**When to Use:** Your team has recurring conflicts or tension that's affecting performance.

**Related Resources:**
- [Conflict Resolution](../leadership/conflict-resolution.md)
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)
- [Difficult Conversations](../leadership/difficult-conversations.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
My team has recurring conflict around [specific issue or pattern].

Details:
- The pattern: [describe what keeps happening]
- Who's involved: [specific people / entire team / subgroups]
- Impact: [how it affects delivery, morale, quality]
- What you've tried: [previous interventions]
- Team history: [new team / long-standing / recently merged / etc.]

Help me:
1. Diagnose the type of conflict (task, process, relationship, values)
2. Identify root causes
3. Design intervention (team workshop, mediation, process change, etc.)
4. Create new team norms if needed
5. Prevent recurrence

Reference the conflict resolution guide for mediation techniques and conflict types.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Conflict type diagnosis
- Root cause analysis
- Intervention plan
- New team norms or processes
- Prevention strategy

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Have team conversation or mediation
- Establish new norms
- Monitor for improvement
- Address quickly if it recurs

## Team Formation (Forming Stage)

**When to Use:** You're building a new team or have significant new members joining.

**Related Resources:**
- [Onboarding](../leadership/onboarding.md)
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)
- [Team Topologies](../practices/team-topologies.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
I'm forming a new team / significantly changing team composition.

Team details:
- Team purpose: [mission, value stream, product area]
- Team size: [target number]
- Composition: [new team / mix of existing + new / merged teams]
- Members: [names, roles, backgrounds if known]
- Timeline: [when does team start]
- Challenges: [distributed / different backgrounds / etc.]

Help me:
1. Plan team launch activities (first week, first month)
2. Establish team charter and norms
3. Build psychological safety from day one
4. Clarify roles and responsibilities
5. Set up effective collaboration practices
6. Navigate forming → storming → norming stages

Reference the psychological safety guide and onboarding guide for team formation best practices.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Team launch plan (first day, week, month)
- Team charter template
- Initial team norms
- Role clarity
- Collaboration practices
- Expectations for forming/storming phases

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Facilitate team charter session
- Set up collaboration tools
- Schedule regular check-ins
- Be prepared for storming
- Invest in team bonding

## Incident Post-Mortem Facilitation

**When to Use:** After a significant incident, you need to facilitate a blameless post-mortem.

**Related Resources:**
- [Incident Management](../practices/incident-management.md)
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
We had an incident and need to run a post-mortem.

Incident details:
- What happened: [brief description]
- Impact: [downtime, customer effect, revenue impact]
- Who was involved: [responders, teams affected]
- Immediate fix: [what was done]
- Emotions: [team stressed / blame starting / defensive / etc.]

Help me facilitate a blameless post-mortem:
1. Structure the session (timeline, 5 whys, etc.)
2. Set the tone for blamelessness
3. Key questions to ask
4. How to identify systemic issues vs individual mistakes
5. Generate actionable follow-up items
6. Build psychological safety through the process

Reference the incident management guide for post-mortem structure and psychological safety guide for blameless culture.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Post-mortem session structure
- Opening statement emphasizing blamelessness
- Discussion questions
- Root cause analysis approach
- Action item template
- Follow-up process

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Schedule post-mortem session
- Facilitate with blameless tone
- Document findings and actions
- Track action completion
- Share learnings with broader org

**Follow-Up Actions:**

- Adjust meeting cadence

## Team Performance Slump

**When to Use:** Team velocity or performance has noticeably declined.

**Related Resources:**
- [Team Health Metrics](../practices/team-health-metrics.md)
- [Burnout Prevention](../heuristics/burnout-prevention.md)
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
[Team name]'s performance has declined recently.

Observable data:
- Metrics: [deployment frequency down / lead time up / more incidents / lower velocity]
- Timeline: [when decline started]
- Recent events: [team changes, incidents, org changes, projects completed, etc.]
- Team signals: [morale, energy level, complaints]
- External factors: [deadlines, pressure, organizational changes]

Help me:
1. Diagnose root cause(s) of performance decline
2. Determine if this is burnout, process issue, skill gap, or something else
3. Create recovery plan
4. Identify quick wins to build momentum
5. Set realistic expectations for recovery timeline

Reference team health metrics, burnout prevention, and psychological safety guides.
```

**Expected Output:**
- Root cause analysis
- Issue categorization (burnout, process, technical debt, etc.)
- Recovery plan with phases
- Quick wins to build momentum
- Realistic timeline and metrics

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Address root causes immediately
- Reduce load if burnout
- Fix process issues
- Celebrate small wins
- Monitor recovery

- Share success story
- Document lessons
- Channel momentum to next goal

## Team Norm Setting

**When to Use:** You need to establish or reset team norms and working agreements.

**Related Resources:**
- [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)
- [Remote/Hybrid Teams](../leadership/remote-hybrid-teams.md)

**The Prompt:**
```
I need to establish team norms for [new team / existing team that needs reset / specific issue].

Context:
- Team situation: [new / existing / recent issues / etc.]
- Specific areas needing norms: [meetings, code review, communication, work hours, etc.]
- Team size: [number]
- Work style: [remote / hybrid / in-office]
- Current pain points: [meeting overload / unclear expectations / etc.]

Help me:
1. Identify which norms to establish (meeting, communication, code review, etc.)
2. Facilitate team discussion to co-create norms
3. Document norms clearly
4. Get team buy-in and commitment
5. Hold team accountable to norms

Reference psychological safety guide for norm-setting and remote teams guide for remote-specific norms.
```

**Expected Output:**
- List of norm areas to address
- Facilitation plan for team discussion
- Draft norms template
- Documentation format
- Accountability mechanisms

**Follow-Up Actions:**
- Facilitate team norm-setting session
- Document agreed norms
- Make visible (wiki, team space)
- Revisit in retrospectives
- Update as needed
