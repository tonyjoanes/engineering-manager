# Onboarding Playbook

A comprehensive guide to ramping up new team members effectively.

## Why Onboarding Matters

**Good onboarding:**
- Accelerates time-to-productivity
- Increases retention (36% higher after 3 years)
- Builds engagement and loyalty
- Establishes culture and expectations
- Creates positive first impression

**Bad onboarding:**
- New hire sits idle with nothing to do
- Overwhelmed without support
- Quits within first 90 days
- Takes 6+ months to contribute

**Your goal:** Make them productive, confident, and connected.

## The 30-60-90 Day Framework

### First 30 Days: Learn
**Focus:** Absorb information, build relationships, ship something small

**Success looks like:**
- Understands team mission and current projects
- Met key people across the org
- Dev environment set up
- Shipped first meaningful contribution
- Asking good questions

### Days 31-60: Contribute
**Focus:** Take on real work with guidance

**Success looks like:**
- Owns a feature or project (with support)
- Contributing in team discussions
- Independently handling some tasks
- Understanding team processes
- Building stronger relationships

### Days 61-90: Own
**Focus:** Increasing autonomy and impact

**Success looks like:**
- Independently shipping work
- Proactively identifying improvements
- Mentoring newer team members
- Solid understanding of broader context
- Fully productive team member

## Day One

### Morning (First 4 hours)

**9:00am - Manager Meeting (30 min)**
- Welcome them warmly
- Walk through first day schedule
- Share team handbook/docs
- Set expectations for first week
- Answer immediate questions

**9:30am - HR/Admin (60 min)**
- Paperwork
- Benefits enrollment
- Company policies
- Security briefing

**10:30am - Environment Setup (90 min)**
- Computer setup
- Dev environment
- Access to repos/tools
- Slack channels joined
- Email configured

**12:00pm - Team Lunch**
- Informal setting
- Get to know teammates
- No work talk required

### Afternoon (Next 4 hours)

**1:00pm - Team Introductions (60 min)**
- Each person introduces themselves
- What they work on
- Fun fact
- How to contact them

**2:00pm - Codebase Tour (60 min)**
- Architecture overview
- Where things live
- How to run locally
- How to run tests
- First "hello world" commit

**3:00pm - Onboarding Buddy Time (60 min)**
- Answer questions
- Show them around (if in office)
- Cultural norms
- Unwritten rules

**4:00pm - End of Day Check-in (15 min)**
- How did it go?
- Any blockers for tomorrow?
- Reinforce that questions are welcome
- Celebrate their first day

### First Day Don'ts

❌ Don't schedule back-to-back meetings all day
❌ Don't expect them to remember everything
❌ Don't leave them alone with no guidance
❌ Don't throw them into deep technical work
❌ Don't skip lunch or informal time

## First Week

### Day 2-5 Goals

**Technical:**
- Complete dev environment setup
- Read key documentation
- Make first real code contribution
- Understand deployment process
- Know how to get help

**Process:**
- Understand sprint cycle
- Know how standups work
- Learn code review process
- Understand ticket workflow

**People:**
- 1:1s with each immediate teammate
- Meet key stakeholders
- Know who to ask for what
- Join team social channels

### First Week Schedule Template

**Daily Structure:**
- Morning: Learning/reading time
- Late morning: Pairing or guided work
- Lunch: With different team members
- Afternoon: Independent work (with buddy available)
- End of day: Check-in with buddy or manager

**Meetings to Schedule:**
- Daily standup (observe first, participate by day 3)
- 1:1 with manager (day 2)
- 1:1s with teammates (spread across week)
- Team lunch or social activity
- Architecture overview session

**Tasks to Assign:**
- Day 2: Fix a typo in docs
- Day 3: Fix a small bug
- Day 4: Add a small feature
- Day 5: First PR review participation

### First Week Check-in Questions

**Ask them:**
- What's been most surprising?
- What's been most confusing?
- Who have you enjoyed meeting?
- What would make this week better?
- What questions do you have?

**Assess:**
- Are they asking questions?
- Do they seem overwhelmed or bored?
- Are they connecting with team?
- Is technical setup working?
- Adjust plan if needed

## First Month

### Week 2-4 Focus Areas

**Technical Growth:**
- Own small features end-to-end
- Participate in code reviews
- Understand testing strategy
- Learn debugging techniques
- Know how to find information

**Team Integration:**
- Contributing in meetings
- Collaborating with others
- Understanding team dynamics
- Building working relationships

**Process Mastery:**
- Comfortable with workflows
- Knows when to ask for help
- Understands quality standards
- Can navigate bureaucracy

### Onboarding Buddy Responsibilities

**Assign a buddy who:**
- Is not their manager
- Has 1-2 years with the company
- Is friendly and approachable
- Has time to dedicate

**Buddy responsibilities:**
- Daily check-ins first week
- Answer "stupid questions"
- Explain unwritten rules
- Introduce to people
- Coffee/lunch hangouts
- Be their advocate

**Time commitment:** 5-7 hours in first week, 2-3 hours per week after

### First Month Projects

**Characteristics of good starter projects:**
- Real work, not busy work
- Well-scoped and achievable
- Has clear success criteria
- Not blocking critical path
- Touches multiple parts of codebase
- Has an experienced mentor available

**Examples:**
- Implement small feature with specs
- Improve error handling in area X
- Add monitoring to service Y
- Refactor component Z
- Write tests for legacy code

**Anti-examples:**
- Critical path work
- Vague scope ("improve performance")
- Work nobody else wants to do
- Solo project with no guidance

### 30-Day Check-in

**Schedule formal 30-day review:**

**Topics to cover:**
- How's it going overall?
- What's going well?
- What's been challenging?
- How's the team fit?
- Any surprises?
- Is role matching expectations?
- What support do you need?

**Manager assessment:**
- Are they where you expect?
- Any concerns emerging?
- Need to adjust anything?
- Clear on next 30 days?

## Months 2-3

### Increasing Autonomy

**Week 5-8:**
- Leading features (with review)
- Taking on-call responsibilities
- Participating in planning
- Contributing to architecture discussions

**Week 9-12:**
- Independent project ownership
- Mentoring newer members
- Process improvement ideas
- Full team citizenship

### 60-Day Check-in

**Questions:**
- Feeling productive and effective?
- Clear on expectations?
- Building relationships?
- Understanding bigger picture?
- Career goals aligning?

### 90-Day Review

**Formal performance conversation:**

**Evaluate:**
- Technical contributions
- Collaboration and communication
- Culture fit and values
- Growth trajectory
- Areas for development

**Discuss:**
- Feedback on performance
- Adjustment to role expectations
- Next 6 months goals
- Career development plan
- Any concerns either side

**Outcomes:**
- Clear expectations going forward
- Development plan in place
- Mutual understanding of fit
- Adjust if needed

## Remote Onboarding Considerations

### Additional Challenges
- Harder to build relationships
- Can't read body language
- Feels isolated
- Misses casual conversations
- Technology barriers

### Remote-Specific Practices

**Over-communicate:**
- More frequent check-ins
- Video calls > messages
- Don't assume they're okay

**Create Connection:**
- Virtual coffee chats
- Slack channels for socializing
- Team virtual events
- Pair programming sessions

**Be Explicit:**
- Document everything
- Record meetings
- Share context proactively
- Don't rely on "overhearing"

**Ship them swag:**
- Company swag
- Welcome package
- Makes them feel part of team

## Common Onboarding Mistakes

### ❌ Drinking from Firehose
- Too much info too fast
- **Fix:** Pace it out, prioritize what's critical

### ❌ Sink or Swim
- No structure or support
- **Fix:** Clear plan and dedicated buddy

### ❌ No Real Work
- Days of just reading docs
- **Fix:** Ship something small in first week

### ❌ Ignored After Day 1
- Great first day, then forgotten
- **Fix:** Scheduled check-ins through 90 days

### ❌ No Feedback
- Waiting for 90-day review
- **Fix:** Continuous feedback from day one

### ❌ Wrong Expectations
- Expect immediate productivity
- **Fix:** Accept 3-month ramp-up time

## Measuring Onboarding Success

### Metrics to Track
- Time to first commit (goal: day 2-3)
- Time to first meaningful contribution (goal: week 1)
- Time to independent work (goal: month 2)
- 30/60/90 day retention
- New hire satisfaction survey
- Time to full productivity (goal: 90 days)

### Survey Questions (30 days)

Rate 1-5:
- I have the tools and resources I need
- I understand my role and expectations
- I feel welcomed by the team
- I'm learning and growing
- I'm confident in my ability to succeed
- I would recommend working here

**Open-ended:**
- What's been most helpful?
- What could be improved?
- What do you still need?

## Manager Self-Assessment

After each onboarding:

**What went well?**
- What should I repeat?
- What did they appreciate most?

**What could improve?**
- Where did they struggle?
- What would have helped?
- What will I do differently next time?

**Update the playbook** - Onboarding should get better each time.
