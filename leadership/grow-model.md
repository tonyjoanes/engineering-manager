# GROW Model

The GROW model is a simple but powerful framework for structuring coaching conversations and goal-setting.

## Overview

**GROW** stands for:
- **G**oal
- **R**eality
- **O**ptions
- **W**ay Forward (or Will)

## The Framework

### 1. Goal
*What do you want to achieve?*

Questions to ask:
- What would you like to discuss?
- What would you like to achieve from this conversation?
- What does success look like?
- How will you know you've achieved it?

### 2. Reality
*Where are you now?*

Questions to ask:
- What's happening now?
- What have you tried so far?
- What's working? What's not?
- What obstacles are you facing?
- Who else is involved?

### 3. Options
*What could you do?*

Questions to ask:
- What options do you have?
- What else could you try?
- What would you do if [constraint] wasn't an issue?
- What has worked in similar situations?
- What would you advise a friend to do?

### 4. Way Forward
*What will you do?*

Questions to ask:
- What are you going to do?
- When will you do it?
- What might get in the way?
- How will you overcome obstacles?
- How committed are you? (scale of 1-10)
- What support do you need?

## Tips for Using GROW

- Keep questions open-ended
- Listen more than you talk
- Don't jump to solutions
- Let the person come to their own conclusions
- Ensure commitment before ending
