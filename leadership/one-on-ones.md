# One-on-One Meetings

A guide to conducting effective and constructive 1:1 meetings with your team members.

## Why 1:1s Matter

- **Primary communication channel** between manager and team member
- **Build trust** through regular, dedicated time
- **Early problem detection** before issues escalate
- **Career development** and coaching opportunity
- **Two-way feedback** exchange

## Core Principles

1. **Consistency is key** - Don't cancel or reschedule frequently
2. **Their meeting, not yours** - They set the agenda
3. **Listen more than you talk** - 70/30 split
4. **Take notes** - Shows you care and enables follow-up
5. **Create psychological safety** - Make it a judgment-free zone

## Meeting Structure

### Recommended Format (30 minutes)

**First 5 minutes: Personal/Team Updates**
- How are you doing?
- Team dynamics and morale check
- Recent wins or challenges

**Next 15 minutes: Their Agenda**
- Current work and blockers
- Questions and concerns
- Ideas and suggestions
- Whatever they want to discuss

**Next 7 minutes: Your Topics**
- Updates they should know
- Feedback (both positive and constructive)
- Requests or asks

**Last 3 minutes: Wrap-up**
- Recap action items
- Confirm next meeting
- Any final thoughts

### Shared Document Best Practices

- Use a running doc (don't create new docs each time)
- Both add topics before the meeting
- Take notes during the meeting in the doc
- Review action items from previous meetings
- Keep history visible for context

## Question Banks

### General Check-in Questions
- What's on your mind?
- What's going well? What's not?
- What can I do to help you this week?
- Is there anything blocking you?
- What's one thing I could do differently?

### Work & Projects
- What are you most excited about working on?
- What feels like a grind?
- Do you feel you have enough context on your projects?
- Are priorities clear?
- What's something you'd like to work on that you're not?

### Team & Collaboration
- How's your relationship with [teammate]?
- Is there anyone you'd like to work more closely with?
- Anyone you'd like to learn from?
- How do you feel about team meetings/ceremonies?
- What's the team energy like from your perspective?

### Growth & Development
- What skills do you want to develop?
- What would make you feel more effective?
- Is there anything you'd like to learn?
- What kind of work energizes you?
- What are your career goals?

### Manager Effectiveness
- What do you need more of from me? Less of?
- How can I better support you?
- Am I giving you enough context?
- Is my feedback helpful?
- What's one thing I could improve?

### Big Picture
- Do you understand how your work connects to company goals?
- Do you feel your work is valued?
- What would you change about how we work?
- Where do you see yourself in a year?

## Using GROW Framework in 1:1s

When a team member brings up a challenge, use the [GROW model](./grow-model.md):

1. **Goal**: What do you want to achieve?
2. **Reality**: Where are you now? What's the situation?
3. **Options**: What could you do? What are alternatives?
4. **Way Forward**: What will you do? When?

## Different Types of 1:1s

### Weekly/Regular 1:1s
- Focus: Day-to-day work, immediate concerns
- Length: 30 minutes
- Keep it conversational and flexible

### Monthly Career 1:1s
- Focus: Long-term development, career goals
- Length: 45-60 minutes
- More structured, use specific frameworks

### Skip-Level 1:1s
- Focus: Broader perspective, team health
- Be clear you won't break manager's trust
- Ask about team dynamics and manager effectiveness

### Performance Review 1:1s
- Focus: Past performance, future expectations
- No surprises - should reflect ongoing feedback
- Use specific examples and data

## Making 1:1s Constructive

### DO
✓ **Start with open-ended questions**
✓ **Be present** - close laptop, silence phone
✓ **Probe deeper** - ask "tell me more" or "why?"
✓ **Share context** - help them see the bigger picture
✓ **Give specific feedback** - use examples
✓ **Follow up on action items** - builds trust
✓ **Vary the location** - coffee shop, walk, etc.

### DON'T
✗ **Make it a status update** - use async for status
✗ **Dominate the conversation** - you're listening
✗ **Skip regularly** - shows it's not a priority
✗ **Ambush with feedback** - they should feel safe
✗ **Forget to take notes** - you'll forget important things
✗ **Use it only for problems** - celebrate wins too

## Common Pitfalls

### "We have nothing to talk about"
- They haven't prepared - send agenda reminder beforehand
- Too frequent - consider bi-weekly instead
- Need to build trust - ask deeper questions
- Status updates taking all the time - move to async

### Surface-level conversations
- Not enough psychological safety yet
- Ask more personal questions
- Share your own vulnerabilities
- Give them time to think (embrace silence)

### Always running over time
- Not timeboxing topics
- Going too deep on everything
- Need separate meetings for some topics
- Start on time, even if they're late

### Constantly rescheduling
- Signals low priority to team member
- Damages trust
- Set recurring time and protect it

## Action Items and Follow-up

### Tracking Action Items
- Assign owner (you or them)
- Set deadline or "by next 1:1"
- Review at start of next meeting
- If repeatedly delayed, discuss why

### Sample Action Item Format
```
- [ ] [Person] will [specific action] by [date]
- [ ] Follow up on [topic] next meeting
```

## Red Flags to Watch For

- **Energy shift** - suddenly less engaged
- **Vague answers** - avoiding something
- **Stopped bringing topics** - lost trust or motivation
- **Only talks about work** - not opening up
- **Mentions leaving** - even jokingly, probe deeper

## Frequency Guidelines

| Relationship | Frequency |
|--------------|-----------|
| New team member (first 3 months) | Weekly |
| Regular team members | Weekly or Bi-weekly |
| Senior/Staff engineers | Bi-weekly |
| Career development deep-dives | Monthly |
| Skip levels | Quarterly |

## Sample Agenda Templates

### For New Team Member
```
- How's onboarding going?
- What's clear? What's confusing?
- Who have you connected with?
- What questions do you have?
- What can I do to help you ramp up?
```

### For Struggling Performer
```
- How are you feeling about your work?
- What obstacles are you facing?
- What support would help?
- Let's review specific examples
- Action plan for improvement
```

### For High Performer
```
- What's keeping you engaged?
- What's next for your growth?
- What problems do you want to solve?
- How can I give you more autonomy?
- What would make you consider leaving?
```
