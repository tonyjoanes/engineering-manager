# Avoiding Burnout

Preventing and recovering from burnout for yourself and your team.

## What is Burnout?

**WHO Definition:** "Chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed"

**Three dimensions:**
1. **Exhaustion** - Feeling drained and depleted
2. **Cynicism** - Negative attitude toward work
3. **Reduced efficacy** - Feeling incompetent and ineffective

**Not the same as:**
- Stress (short-term, recoverable)
- Being tired (sleep helps)
- Having a bad day/week

**Burnout is systemic and requires intervention.**

## Signs of Burnout

### Physical Signs
- Constant fatigue
- Insomnia or oversleeping
- Headaches
- Stomach issues
- Getting sick often
- Changes in appetite

### Emotional Signs
- Cynicism and negativity
- Irritability and anger
- Feeling helpless
- Loss of motivation
- Dread going to work
- Emotional numbness

### Behavioral Signs
- Procrastination
- Withdrawing from others
- Decreased performance
- Missing work
- Using substances to cope
- Taking frustration out on others

### Cognitive Signs
- Can't concentrate
- Forgetfulness
- Inability to make decisions
- Lack of creativity
- Negative self-talk

**If experiencing multiple signs for weeks: you're burning out.**

## Causes of Burnout

### Work-Related Causes

**Overwork:**
- Too much work
- Unrealistic deadlines
- No work-life boundaries
- Always-on culture

**Lack of Control:**
- No autonomy
- Micromanagement
- Can't influence decisions
- Powerlessness

**Insufficient Reward:**
- Low compensation
- No recognition
- Lack of appreciation
- Effort unnoticed

**Lack of Community:**
- Isolation
- Toxic coworkers
- No support
- Conflict

**Absence of Fairness:**
- Favoritism
- Unequal treatment
- Broken promises
- Politics

**Value Conflicts:**
- Work conflicts with values
- Ethical concerns
- Meaningless work
- Wrong fit

### Personal Factors

**Perfectionism:**
- Never good enough
- Can't delegate
- Overcommit

**People-pleasing:**
- Can't say no
- Everyone else's needs first
- Boundary issues

**High achiever:**
- Never satisfied
- Always more to do
- Rest feels like failure

**Life circumstances:**
- Family stress
- Health issues
- Financial pressure
- Major life changes

## Preventing Your Own Burnout

### 1. Set Boundaries

**Work hours:**
- Define start and end
- Stick to them
- Communicate them
- No guilt

**After hours:**
- Don't check email
- Only emergencies
- Phone off
- Protect evenings/weekends

**Vacation:**
- Take all your PTO
- Unplug completely
- Don't feel guilty
- Trust your team

### 2. Prioritize Ruthlessly

**You can't do everything:**
- Say no more
- Delegate more
- Good enough is enough
- Focus on what matters

**Use [Eisenhower Matrix](./time-management.md):**
- Important & Urgent: Do
- Important & Not Urgent: Schedule
- Urgent & Not Important: Delegate
- Not Urgent & Not Important: Eliminate

### 3. Protect Your Energy

**Know your limits:**
- How much is sustainable?
- What drains you?
- What energizes you?
- Plan accordingly

**Energy management:**
- Match tasks to energy levels
- Schedule recovery time
- Don't stack draining activities
- Build in buffers

### 4. Take Care of Physical Health

**Non-negotiables:**
- Sleep 7-8 hours
- Exercise 3-4x per week
- Eat reasonably well
- Stay hydrated
- Get outside

**When burned out, these slip first - protect them.**

### 5. Maintain Social Connections

**At work:**
- Peer managers
- Mentors
- Friends on other teams
- Support network

**Outside work:**
- Family
- Friends
- Hobbies
- Community

**Isolation worsens burnout.**

### 6. Find Meaning

**Connect work to impact:**
- Who does it help?
- What difference does it make?
- Why does it matter?

**If can't find meaning:**
- Might be wrong role
- Might need change
- Talk to manager

### 7. Practice Self-Compassion

**You're human:**
- Mistakes are OK
- You can't do everything
- Rest isn't laziness
- Good enough is good

**Challenge negative self-talk:**
- Would you say this to a friend?
- Is it really true?
- What's a more balanced view?

### 8. Regular Check-ins

**Monthly self-assessment:**
- Energy level?
- Enjoying work?
- Sleeping well?
- Physical health?
- Relationships OK?
- Feeling effective?

**If declining:** Act early.

## Preventing Team Burnout

### Recognize the Signs

**Watch for:**
- Declining performance
- Increased errors
- Missed deadlines
- Withdrawal
- Cynicism
- Sick days up
- People leaving

**Individual signals:**
- Always tired
- Negative attitude
- Quality dropping
- Avoiding people
- Not themselves

**Act early - easier to prevent than fix.**

### Create Sustainable Pace

**Avoid:**
- Death marches
- Constant crunch
- No time off
- Always urgent

**Sustain:**
- Reasonable workload
- Clear priorities
- Time for slack
- Regular rhythm

**Marathon, not sprint.**

### Give Control & Autonomy

**Autonomy prevents burnout:**
- Let people choose how
- Input on what
- Flexible schedules
- Trust and verify

**Micromanagement causes burnout:**
- Constant oversight
- No trust
- Every decision approved

### Recognize & Appreciate

**People need to feel valued:**
- Recognize contributions
- Celebrate wins
- Thank people
- Show impact of work

See [Recognition](../leadership/recognition.md)

### Build Community

**Strong teams resist burnout:**
- Psychological safety
- Support each other
- Have fun together
- Care about each other

See [Psychological Safety](../practices/psychological-safety.md)

### Ensure Fairness

**Fairness matters:**
- Equal treatment
- Clear criteria
- Keep promises
- No favoritism
- Transparent decisions

### Provide Growth

**Stagnation leads to burnout:**
- Learning opportunities
- Career development
- New challenges
- Skill building

See [Career Development](../leadership/career-development.md)

### Model Healthy Behavior

**They watch you:**
- Take vacation
- Set boundaries
- Take breaks
- Admit limits
- Prioritize health

**If you're burning out, they will too.**

## Addressing Active Burnout

### For Yourself

**If you're burned out:**

**1. Acknowledge it**
- Not weakness
- Not personal failure
- Systemic issue
- Needs addressing

**2. Tell someone**
- Your manager
- HR
- Coach/therapist
- Trusted friend

**3. Take time off**
- At least a week
- Preferably 2-3 weeks
- Truly unplug
- Don't work

**4. Identify root causes**
- What's driving it?
- What needs to change?
- What's in your control?

**5. Make changes**
- Set boundaries
- Delegate more
- Say no more
- Change role?
- Leave job?

**6. Get help**
- Therapy
- Coach
- Doctor
- Support group

**Don't power through - it gets worse.**

### For a Team Member

**If someone's burning out:**

**1. Have the conversation**
- Private setting
- Express concern
- Ask how they're doing
- Really listen

**Script:**
"I've noticed [specific observations]. I'm concerned about you. How are you really doing?"

**2. Validate their experience**
- It's not in their head
- Their feelings are real
- Not a personal failure
- Thank them for being honest

**3. Reduce load immediately**
- Take things off plate
- Extend deadlines
- Provide support
- Cover urgent items

**4. Encourage time off**
- Take PTO
- Suggest minimum time
- Full disconnection
- No guilt

**5. Address root causes**
- What's driving it?
- What can change?
- What's in team's control?
- Make concrete changes

**6. Follow up**
- Regular check-ins
- Monitor improvement
- Adjust workload
- Sustained support

**7. Connect to resources**
- EAP (Employee Assistance Program)
- Therapy
- HR
- Health resources

**Don't ignore it or hope it goes away.**

## Recovery from Burnout

### Recovery Timeline

**Mild burnout:** 3-6 months
**Moderate burnout:** 6-12 months
**Severe burnout:** 1-2 years

**It takes time - be patient.**

### Recovery Strategies

**Rest and recharge:**
- Extended time off
- Reduce commitments
- Say no to new things
- Focus on basics

**Reconnect with what matters:**
- Why did you choose this work?
- What did you used to enjoy?
- What gives you energy?

**Make sustainable changes:**
- Not just vacation
- Systemic fixes
- New habits
- Boundary enforcement

**Build support:**
- Therapy
- Peer support
- Friends and family
- Professional help

**Consider bigger changes:**
- Different role
- Different team
- Different company
- Career change

**Sometimes the environment is the problem.**

## When to Leave

### Signs It's Time to Go

**Consider leaving if:**
- Toxic culture that won't change
- Values misalignment
- Unsupportive management
- Constant overwork
- No path forward
- Affecting health seriously
- Tried everything, still burning out

**Leaving isn't failure:**
- Sometimes the right answer
- Your health matters most
- Better opportunities exist
- Can't fix some environments

### Before Quitting Impulsively

**Try first:**
- Talk to manager
- Request changes
- Take extended leave
- Different role/team

**But don't stay too long:**
- Some situations won't improve
- Your health matters
- It's OK to leave

## Burnout vs. Other Issues

### Depression

**Burnout:**
- Work-specific
- Improves with rest
- Feelings of emptiness
- Caused by work conditions

**Depression:**
- All areas of life
- Doesn't improve with rest
- Feelings of hopelessness
- May have biological causes

**Need professional help to distinguish.**

### Stress

**Stress:**
- Short-term
- Recoverable
- Too much to do
- Feeling pressured

**Burnout:**
- Chronic
- Not recoverable without intervention
- Nothing matters
- Feeling defeated

**Burnout is end stage of chronic stress.**
